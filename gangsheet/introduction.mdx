A **gang sheet** is one length of transfer film carrying many unrelated
designs, packed as tightly as they will go. Shops sell it by the length of
film used; customers fill it with whatever they need pressed. The Gang
Sheet Builder is that experience inside Print.App: a purpose-built editor
where customers upload their designs, say how big and how many, and get a
packed, print-ready sheet without ever touching a canvas tool.

It is a **design type**, like a regular design but with no pages of its
own. You create it in the dashboard, set the film and the rules, assign it
to a product, and the builder opens in place of the studio.

## What customers do

<Steps>
    <Step title="Add designs">
        Upload files, add text in your fonts, or pick from your graphics
        library. Each design becomes a row in a list.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Set size and quantity">
        Width, height and copies per design. The builder checks the print
        resolution as they type and tells them the largest size that
        still passes.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Let it pack">
        Every change re-packs the sheet automatically, rotating pieces to
        save film when allowed. The summary shows the length used and how
        much of the paid length is still free.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Save">
        The sheet saves as a project, the store prices the length, and the
        shop receives a print-ready PDF or PNG with a transparent
        background.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## What it does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
    <Card title="Auto-nesting" icon="table-cells" color="#0BDED2">
        A server-side packer places every copy with your gap and edge
        rules, rotation optional, and validates the layout before checkout.
    </Card>
    <Card title="A resolution gate" icon="magnifying-glass" color="#F203BB">
        Pixels over inches, checked live. Below your floor the design is
        flagged with the size that would pass, and checkout can be blocked.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Design tools" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" color="#eab308">
        Text, gallery graphics, crop and adjust, background removal,
        halftone and keyline outlines — each a switch on the design.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Your store's pricing" icon="tag" color="#22c55e">
        The builder measures the film; your store sets the rate — through
        Print Options with length tiers, or a plain theme input.
    </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Two modes

- **Gang sheet** — the customer builds the sheet and pays for the film
  length. The market's standard product.
- **Transfers by size** — the customer sets a print size and copy count
  per design and pays by print area with copy breaks; the shop nests the
  transfers behind the scenes. The same design, a different mode.

## What the shop receives

A project with one page per sheet, sized to the film width and the billed
length, with every design placed from its original file at native
resolution, text and SVG graphics as vectors, and a transparent
background. Output as PDF or a 300 dpi PNG. See
[Output and production](/gangsheet/output).

<Note>
    The Gang Sheet Builder runs on the new PrintFx editor. Turn on **Switch
    to new Editor** in your dashboard settings before creating one.
</Note>
