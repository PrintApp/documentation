---
title: FAQs
description: "What Template Studio can and cannot do: products, fonts, your logo and brand, finishes, editing, and what to do when a draft is not right."
---

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Is this an AI image generator?">
        No. An image generator gives you a picture. Template Studio builds a
        Print.App design: real pages at the product's size with bleed and a
        safe area, text your customers edit through fields, photo and logo
        frames they fill, locked decoration, and print modules like QR
        codes. You can open it in the editor and change anything, like a
        design you made yourself.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which products can it design?">
        Business cards, cards and invitations, gift tags, menus, funeral
        programs, apparel prints, mug and tumbler wraps, labels and
        stickers, ornaments, gift wrap, banners and posters, signs and
        flags, in the sizes listed on [What is Template Studio](/template-studio/introduction#products).
        If you ask for something else, it tells you it cannot make that
        product yet.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can it use my fonts?">
        Not yet. Each direction uses one of twelve tested font pairings:
        Modern sans, Geometric sans, Classic serif, Elegant serif with
        script, Luxury serif, Editorial, Warm and crafted, Bold display,
        Retro, Playful, Friendly rounded and Slab. If you ask for a font
        that is not among them, a **Good to know** note names the pairing
        used instead. After keeping, you can change fonts in the editor.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can it use my logo, brand colours or graphics?">
        Give your colours in the brief or in **Colours** and the palette
        follows them. Logos go in a logo frame with a placeholder that you
        or your customers replace. Artwork comes from the Print.App
        graphics library; your own uploaded graphics are not used yet.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can it add foil, spot UV or white ink?">
        Not yet. If you ask for a special finish, the design uses an
        ordinary ink instead (gold ink for gold foil, for example) and a
        **Good to know** note tells you.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="A draft is not quite right. What should I do?">
        Type what to change in the note box under the draft and click the
        try-again button: it redraws that draft with your change. With the
        box empty, you get a fresh take on the same direction. Drafts are
        free. If the draft is close, you can also keep it with a note,
        which is applied while it is finished.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What is the difference between a change and editing it myself?">
        A change (three are included with each kept design) is done for you:
        describe it and Template Studio updates the saved design. You can
        also open the design in the editor at any time and edit it
        yourself; that does not use a change.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Where do kept designs go?">
        Into the folder you had open when you started the brief, as
        ordinary designs. Assign them to products like any other design.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How long does it take?">
        About a minute to plan and draft, one to two minutes to finish and
        save a kept design, and about a minute for a change. You can
        minimise the panel and keep working meanwhile.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What happens to drafts I do not keep?">
        They stay in the panel until you click **Start new**, and are not
        charged. Only kept designs are saved to your Designs.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
