Template Studio designs **templates** for your store. You tell it what you
need, in your own words, with a link to a product page or by picking a
product and size. It plans up to three different directions, drafts each
one, and saves the ones you keep into your Designs, ready to assign to a
product.

It is not an image generator. What it makes is a real Print.App design,
built page by page in the editor your customers use:

- **Editable.** Every name, date, phone number and line of copy is a field
  your customers fill in. Logos and photos sit in frames they replace.
  Decoration is locked so a customer cannot knock it out of place.
- **Print-ready.** The product's size, bleed and safe area, folds on folded
  cards and programs, die-cut shapes on round labels and ornaments, and the
  matching cut line. Text stays inside the safe area and large enough to
  read at the product's size.
- **Designed.** A palette, a font pairing and artwork from the Print.App
  graphics library, chosen for the direction, with layouts that suit the
  product: a roll-up banner reads from across a hall, a business card at
  arm's length.

## How it works

<Steps>
    <Step title="Brief">
        Describe the design, paste a product page link, or pick a product,
        size, theme, colours and style. One variation by default, up to
        three.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Directions">
        Template Studio plans a different direction for each variation: a
        name, a look, a palette, a font pairing and the graphics to use.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Drafts">
        Each direction is drafted in about a minute. Drafts are free: try
        again for a fresh take, or add a note and it redraws that draft
        with your change.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Keep">
        Keep the drafts you like. A finishing pass sets up every field and
        lock, tidies the layout, and saves the design into your current
        folder. Each kept design includes three changes you can ask for
        afterwards.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## What it builds in

<CardGroup cols={2}>
    <Card title="Customer fields" icon="pen-to-square" color="#0BDED2">
        Names, dates, contact details and messages become titled fields
        with sensible length limits, so the form your customers see is
        ready to use.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Print setup" icon="ruler-combined" color="#F203BB">
        Bleed, safe area, folds, multi-page products and die-cut shapes,
        taken from the product and format you chose.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Print modules" icon="qrcode" color="#FFB800">
        Real, scannable QR codes (one can follow the website your
        customer types) and curved text that follows round products.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Quality checks" icon="list-check" color="#5B5BD6">
        Every draft is checked as it is built: text size and contrast,
        alignment, the safe area, overlapping text. A final review looks
        at the finished pages before you see them.
    </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where to find it

In the admin, open **Designs**, click **Create New Design** and choose
**Template Studio**. See [Creating designs](/template-studio/creating-designs)
for a walkthrough, and [Pricing](/template-studio/pricing) for what it costs:
your first five kept designs are free.
