| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Your store's first 5 kept designs, once (not every month) |
| **Then** | $0.25 per kept design |
| **Included with each kept design** | 3 changes |
| **Drafts and redraws** | Free |

You only pay for a design you **keep**. Planning directions, drafting,
trying again and redrawing a draft with your note cost nothing. A kept
design's three included changes are part of its price.

The panel always shows where you stand: how many free designs are left, and
the price under **Keep this** once they are used.

## How it is billed

Template Studio is billed with the rest of your Print.App usage, as
**Template Studio (AI Designer)** on your usage statement:

- **Print.App invoice:** one line with the number of paid designs kept in
  the billing period (free ones are not listed).
- **Shopify stores:** included in your Print.App usage charge, under the
  usage billing you approved in Shopify.

You can follow it under **Usage and Billing** in the admin.

<Note>
    Keeping a design after your free five needs your billing in good
    standing. On Shopify, you also need to have approved Print.App usage
    billing. If either is missing, Template Studio says so when you click
    **Keep this**, and you can settle it on the **Usage and Billing** page.
    Your free designs are never blocked.
</Note>

## Not charged

- A design whose finishing pass fails is not charged, and does not use up
  a free design.
- Drafts that fail, and drafts you never keep.
- Changes to a kept design (up to the three included).

## Fair use

Each store can start up to 20 briefs or redraws a day.
