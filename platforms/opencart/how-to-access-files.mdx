---
description: "How to access completed order files in OpenCart"
---

In OpenCart, the project files are listed against each order in your admin.

Navigate to `Sales` >> `Orders` and open the order.

Every customized product shows the customer's design previews (click a thumbnail to
open a larger view) and download links for the print-ready **PDF, PNG and JPEG**
files, plus a **Modify Project** link that opens the project in your
[Print.App admin](https://admin.print.app/projects).

<Note>
    Need the TIFF, or the order files in bulk? Open the project in your
    [Print.App admin](https://admin.print.app/projects), where every output format
    is available.
</Note>
