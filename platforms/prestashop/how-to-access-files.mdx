---
description: "How to access completed order files in PrestaShop"
---

In PrestaShop, the project files are listed against each order in your back office.

Navigate to `Orders` >> `Orders` and open the order.

In the product list, every customized line shows a **PrintApp Customization**
block with the customer's design previews and download links for the print-ready
**PDF, PNG, JPEG and TIFF** files, plus a **Modify Project** link that opens the
project in your [Print.App admin](https://admin.print.app/projects).

<Note>
    On the PrestaShop 1.7 build the TIFF link is not available. Download the TIFF
    from the project in your Print.App admin instead.
</Note>

Orders are also synced to Print.App when their status changes to **Processing** or
**Complete**, so you will find them with the buyer attached under your projects as well.
