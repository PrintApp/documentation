Print.App ships the same editor to every platform, but each store integration is
built on that platform's own extension system, so a few features differ. The tables
below show where. Anything not listed works the same everywhere (see
[Available everywhere](#available-everywhere)).

**Legend:** ✅ available · ◐ partial · ❌ not available

## Available everywhere

These work the same on all six platforms and are configured in your
[Print.App settings](https://admin.print.app/settings), not in the store:

- The **Customize** button is added to the product page automatically, no theme edits.
- The customer's **design previews replace the product images** once they save.
- **Display modes**: modal, inline, mini and dialog. See the [Display Modes guide](/configuration/display-modes).
- **Force customization**: hide the add-to-cart button until a design has been saved.
- The **storefront language** is passed to the editor, with your translation overrides applied.
- **Guest customers** can design and order without an account.

## Not yet on any platform

- Editing a design **from the cart page**. Customers return to the product page to
  change a saved design.
- **Order status** pushed back from Print.App to the store. Sync is one-way, store to Print.App.
- **Multi-currency** pricing inside the editor. Prices stay the store's own.
