### Troubleshooting

If the configurator doesn't appear on your product page:

1.  **Check the options are published to that product.** On Shopify, the
    Products page in the app shows which products carry options; on
    Wordpress, check the product's Print Options tab.
2.  **Shopify only — check the theme block.** `Online Store` → `Themes` →
    `Customize` → the product template → the **Print Configurator** block must
    be added once. Without it there is nothing to render.
3.  **Make sure the product is purchasable.** A product that is out of stock or
    unavailable will refuse the add to cart, whatever the options say.
4.  If it still doesn't show, reach out on our
    [Discord community](https://discord.gg/XNdtdFxNdE).

</Note>
