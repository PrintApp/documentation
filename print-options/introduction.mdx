**Print Options** adds a print configurator to your product pages. Your
customers choose the options you sell — paper weight, finish, size, page
count, quantity — watch the price update as they choose, upload their
artwork, and add the configured job to the cart.

It is a separate product from the Print.App **Editor**. The Editor lets
customers *design* artwork; Print Options lets them *specify and price the
job*. They work well together, and neither requires the other.

<Note>
    Print Options is **free**, on every platform it supports.
</Note>

## How it works

1.  **You build an option set** — a named group of choices and pricing rules,
    usually started from a template like Business Cards or Flyers.
2.  **You assign it to products.** One set can serve many products; editing it
    updates all of them at once.
3.  **Your customer configures and adds to cart.** The configurator renders on
    the product page, prices live, and the job's specification travels with the
    order for your production team.
