## The customer's language

Everything the configurator writes itself (*Summary*, *Add to cart*, *Drop
your file here or click to browse*, validation messages, the tax lines) follows
your store's language. Your option names, choices and help text come from your
option set, so write them in your store's language.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Shopify">
    The configurator follows the storefront language, with nothing to set up.
    It speaks **English, Danish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch and
    Romanian**. Storefronts in other languages show English.
</Tab>
<Tab title="WooCommerce">
    Translations for **Danish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch and
    Romanian** ship with the plugin and follow the site language, including
    regional variants such as German (Austria), German (Switzerland), formal
    Dutch and Mexican Spanish. Language packs from translate.wordpress.org
    take precedence when they exist.

    To change any wording, or to add a language:

    - use [Loco Translate](https://wordpress.org/plugins/loco-translate/), or
    - put a `.po`/`.mo` file in `wp-content/languages/plugins`, or
    - change individual labels from code with the `papo_widget_strings`
      filter:

    ```php
    add_filter('papo_widget_strings', function (array $strings): array {
        $strings['summary.addToCart'] = 'Add to basket';
        return $strings;
    });
    ```
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Prices in your store's format

The configurator prints prices exactly as your store does, so the price in
the summary matches the price in the cart.

- **Shopify** uses your store's currency formatting (**Settings** →
  **General**, under the store currency). A format the configurator doesn't
  recognise falls back to the standard format for the customer's language.
- **WooCommerce** uses your currency options (**WooCommerce** → **Settings** →
  **General**): currency symbol, its position, the number of decimals, and the
  thousand and decimal separators. A Romanian store prints *10,00 lei*, just
  as its cart does.

## A note under the price (WooCommerce)

The summary shows your WooCommerce **price display suffix** (**WooCommerce** →
**Settings** → **Tax** → **Price display suffix**) under the total, such as
*incl. VAT*. Placeholders like `{price_including_tax}` are left out. To use
different wording, or to hide the note, return your text (or an empty string)
from the `papo_price_note` filter.

## Showing the tax in the summary

The summary can show how much of the price is tax: the total excluding tax,
the tax and the total including tax, or the tax included in the total. It is
a display only: your store still calculates tax at checkout from the
customer's address, exactly as before.

What the customer sees depends on whether your prices include tax:

| Your prices | The summary shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Exclude tax** | *Total excl. VAT*, then *VAT (20%)*, then *Total incl. VAT* as the headline price. The price per item is shown including tax too. |
| **Include tax** | *Total* as usual, with *Includes VAT (20%): €3.33* under it. |

The tax is named in the customer's language unless you give it a name:
*VAT*, *MwSt.*, *TVA*, *IVA*, *moms* or *btw*. French summaries use *Total
HT* and *Total TTC*. The tax is rounded once and the other lines are derived
from it, so the figures always add up.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Shopify">
    Open the theme editor, select the **Print Configurator** block, and find
    the **Tax** settings:

    | Setting | What it does |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Show tax in the summary** | Turns the breakdown on. |
    | **Tax rate (%)** | Your standard rate, such as `20` or `5.5` (`5,5` and `20%` work too). Without a valid rate between 0 and 100, no breakdown is shown. |
    | **Tax name (optional)** | Leave empty to use the shopper's language: VAT, TVA, MwSt., IVA… |

    Whether your prices include tax comes from Shopify's own **Taxes and
    duties** setting, so the summary can't contradict your checkout.
</Tab>
<Tab title="WooCommerce">
    There is nothing to set up. The breakdown shows when:

    - WooCommerce taxes are enabled,
    - the product is taxable, and
    - prices are displayed **including tax** in the shop (**WooCommerce** →
      **Settings** → **Tax** → **Display prices in the shop**).

    The rate is the one that applies to the shopper's location (or your
    store's base location), for the product's tax class. When exactly one rate
    applies, its own name is used (*MwSt.*, *GST*). Whether your prices are
    entered with or without tax picks the layout above. Stores with compound
    tax rates, and stores that display prices excluding tax, see no breakdown.

    To change the rate or name, or to hide the breakdown on some products,
    use the `papo_tax_display` filter (return an empty array for none).
</Tab>
</Tabs>
