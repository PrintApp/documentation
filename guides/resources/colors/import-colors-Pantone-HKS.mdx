---
title: Import Pantone & HKS Colors
description: "How to import Pantone and HKS spot colors into your Print.App Domain"
---

Print.App lets you import industry-standard **Pantone** and **HKS** spot colors directly into your color collections for use in the editor.

To get started, navigate to the [Colors Page](https://admin.print.app/colors) in your Print.App Admin. You will see the **Import Pantone Colors** and **Import HKS Colors** buttons at the top of the page.

<Frame>
    <img class="rounded-md" src="/images/import-colors.png" />
</Frame>

## Import Pantone Colors

Click the **Import Pantone Colors** button to open the import dialog.

<Frame>
    <img class="rounded-md" src="/images/import-pantone.png" />
</Frame>

1. Select a **Pantone paper stock** from the dropdown (e.g. Paperstock, Coated, Uncoated etc.)
2. Enter your Pantone color codes in the text field as **comma-delimited values**. For example: `186, 226, 298, 375, 431`

<Warning>
Do NOT include the postfix letter (C, U etc.), just the numbers.
You can import a maximum of **50** colors at a time.
</Warning>

3. Click **Submit** to import the colors into your collection.

## Import HKS Colors

Click the **Import HKS Colors** button to open the import dialog.

<Frame>
    <img class="rounded-md" src="/images/import-hks.png" />
</Frame>

Select a **paper stock** from the dropdown. The available HKS paper stocks are:

| Paper Stock | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| HKS Paperstock | Standard HKS paper stock |
| K - Kunstdruckpapier | Coated art paper |
| N - Naturpapier | Natural / uncoated paper |
| E - Endlospapier | Continuous paper |
| Z - Zeitungspapier | Newsprint paper |

Once you select a paper stock, click the Submit button and the full set of HKS colors for that stock will be imported into a new color collection.

<Tip>
After importing, your Pantone and HKS colors will appear as a color collection on the Colors page. You can then assign them to specific Design Templates using a Resource Profile.

[Read more about assigning resources here](/guides/designs/assign-resources)
</Tip>
